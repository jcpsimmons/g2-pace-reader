# Pace Reader Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-04

Pace Reader stores the text you paste and your reading progress in the Even app's local storage. It does not create an account, transmit reading text to an app-controlled server, run analytics, show advertising, or sell personal data.

The app requests no network, microphone, camera, location, or album permission.

You can replace a saved reading at any time by starting a new one. App uninstall and host storage behavior are controlled by the Even platform and the phone operating system.

Questions can be submitted through the public issue tracker for this repository. Do not include private reading text in an issue.
