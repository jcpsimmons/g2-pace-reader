# Pace Reader Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-04

Pace Reader stores imported EPUB text, book metadata, pasted text, and a separate reading position and speed for each book locally in Even app storage and the phone companion's browser storage. EPUB parsing happens on the device. It does not create an account, transmit reading material to an app-controlled server, run analytics, show advertising, or sell personal data.

The app requests no network, microphone, camera, location, or album permission.

You can delete an imported book and its saved position from the Pace Reader library at any time. App uninstall and host storage behavior are controlled by the Even platform and the phone operating system.

Questions can be submitted through the public issue tracker for this repository. Do not include private reading text in an issue.
