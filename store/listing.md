# Pace Reader store listing

## Name

Pace Reader

## Short description

Rolling three-word paced reading for Even G2 with a 100 to 300 WPM base pace.

## Description

Pace Reader presents text as a rolling window of three words. Set a base pace from 100 to 300 words per minute and adjust it to your reading conditions. Punctuation and long words stay visible a little longer. Text is stored in the Even app's local storage. The app has no account or app-controlled network service and requests no network, microphone, camera, location, or album permission.

Stationary use only. Do not use while walking, cycling, driving, or operating machinery. Real-hardware update cadence is pending beta verification.

## Privacy summary

Local app storage. No special permissions. No account required. No app-controlled network service.

## First release notes

Read pasted text in a rolling three-word view with an adjustable 100 to 300 WPM base pace, pause, rewind, restart, and no special permissions.
